FLIGHT SCHOOL

HOW TO FLY

1 · BANK

Turn your device left or right exactly as you do in TURN. The aircraft banks into the turn.

2 · CLIMB & DIVE

Tilt the top edge up to climb and down to dive. Recalibrate if your resting position changes. Invert pitch is available in Pause.

3 · AIRSPEED

Hold Thrust to speed up and Air Brake to slow down. If STALL appears, lower the nose or add thrust.

4 · THE ROUTE

Fly through the pink gate. Yellow gates show what comes later. The first course starts at Midlanda and stretches east past the Strind area.

KEYBOARD: Arrow keys or W/A/S/D fly · Space/R adds thrust · Shift/F slows · C recalibrates · Escape pauses.

ABOUT TURN UP

TURN UP is a small arcade flight simulator built from TURN’s production motion and platform engine. It uses the same iPad steering profile and adds a second tilt axis for pitch.

REAL-WORLD MAP

The Midlanda, Söråker and Strind-area landscape is rendered with MapLibre GL JS, OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenMapTiles and open terrain elevation tiles hosted through the AWS Registry of Open Data.

The route identifies only the general Strind area; it does not place a marker on a private home.

AIRCRAFT
