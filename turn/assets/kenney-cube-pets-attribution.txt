TURN loads a pinned copy of the cat model from:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/satoLG/defend_the_crystal@99136ff0d498d327d912528be5c1931d24d6a8d1/apps/web/public/models/pets/animal-cat.glb

The model is recoloured and supplemented with simple geometry to resemble Bella. TURN includes a procedural fallback cat so the discovery remains available if the external model cannot be loaded.
