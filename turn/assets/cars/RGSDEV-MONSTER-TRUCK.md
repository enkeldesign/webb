# RGSDev Monster Truck

TURN's `monster-truck-rgsdev.glb` is the Monster Truck from **Free Low Poly Vehicles Pack** by Raphael Gonçalves (Rgsdev).

Source: https://rgsdev.itch.io/free-low-poly-vehicles-pack

The source pack's `License.txt` places the assets under CC0: public domain and free to use in commercial and non-commercial projects; credit is not required.

TURN previously evaluated the complete vehicle pack. This release retains only the Monster Truck selected by visual review. It is stored as a conventional standalone GLB instead of the earlier multi-car compressed runtime bundle. The source triangle topology and these semantic material divisions are preserved:

- primary body
- fixed black body/roll-cage trim
- windows
- headlights and rear lights
- suspension/body trim
- tyres
- wheel rims

PAINTJOB changes the primary body and wheel rims plus the selected suspension accent. Windows, lamps, black trim and tyres retain the source presentation.
