The material of TURN.

Shipped tokens

Start with semantic roles, then use primitives when a component needs a specific primitive. The shared token files are the production source of truth.

System sans

Heavy, compact headings and uppercase control labels. Use comfortable sentence case and line height for instructions and documentation.

Authored size ≠ rendered size everywhere.

TURN’s historical UI normalizer scales many px and rem values to 75%. responsive.css, marked data-turn-responsive, uses CSS pixels directly. Check the rendered target, not just its declaration. Use at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels for the controls documented here.

Use the rendered-size token.

--turn-target-min is 44px in design-scale.css, loaded outside the 0.75 normalizer. A var(--turn-target-min) reference stays 44px in both scaled and unscaled styles. Use this token instead of introducing a second 59px authored-size token.

Rendered size

03 / Controls

Recognisable before you read.

Shipped roles

Pink moves forward. Paper provides utilities. Green marks achievement. Orange leaves or changes context. The following buttons demonstrate styling; they do not launch game actions.

One button shape.

Action buttons are rounded rectangles: --turn-radius-control, a 3px Ink border, --turn-shadow-control, at least 44px tall (controls.css). Pressed moves 2px into the shadow. Pills stay for chips, tags and toggles (SHOW RECORDS, PERK, SHIFT, difficulty, ACHIEVEMENTS filters). The round orange close is the one dismiss shape (TURN 1.23.9).

One forward action, one dock.

Each screen has at most one Pink forward action, and it lives in the action dock (action-dock.css): 56px tall (48px on landscape screens under 500px tall), full width on a Paper bar in portrait, bottom-right elsewhere. Home RACE and The Lot’s RACE THIS CAR share its resting, hover (2px lift), pressed and focus (4px Ink ring) states. Shape and colour come from the primary-action role in design-semantic.css.

Action hierarchyPress or tab through the examples

Primary actions are pill-shaped. How to Play is a neutral Home utility, even though the information inside may use blue.

04 / Notices & feedback

The right message.
The right moment.

A recommendation with room around it

The notice card uses an Ink border, a 13.5px radius and a 4.5px hard Ink shadow in its current unscaled stylesheet, a cyan information fill and a close target of at least 44 rendered pixels. The cyan notice appears on Home and The Lot for touch devices in portrait. It reserves space above the menu, hides in landscape and during racing, and remains dismissed for the current session.

It does not lock orientation or block play. Close uses the orange navigation role. The production notice now uses the round orange dialog close control at 44 × 44 CSS pixels.

Separate from the first-race hint

The first race handoff can recommend landscape in loading or staging. It never takes focus. The staged fallback clears after five seconds or when driving starts. A landscape first handoff also consumes the session hint.

This portrait notice is temporary. When portrait polish is complete, remove its JS/CSS, the loading hooks in app.js, its browser test and that test’s workflow step. Review the separate race hint’s copy at the same time.

05 / Race HUD

Leave room for the road.

Schematic compositions

Landscape spreads information along the edges. Portrait creates two lower corner readouts with gauges growing upward. Handedness mirrors the complete control arrangement.

Compare the mirrored layout

The diagrams demonstrate manual steering. With device steering, omit the STEER pad and keep the driving actions on the selected side. Diagram dimensions are illustrative, not touch-target specifications.

Steady readouts

Portrait stat chips are a fixed grid (SPEED · LAP · POSITION, then TIME · BEST) with tabular digits, so a growing value never moves a chip. BOOST sits directly above the drive pad, centred on it for either hand. Race utilities stay 44px tall and never overlap the pad (TURN 1.23.7).

Unified drive pad

DRIFT, BOOST, GAS and stopping-only BRAKE share one block. LOCK, SHIFT and REVERSE attach contextually. Their labels stay readable when the composition mirrors.

Keep release, cancellation and keyboard ownership connected to the production input system. Opening an overlay must release held driving input.

DRIFT and FLOW scorekeeper

Scorekeeper paper contains score, multiplier and lap context. Presentation updates are throttled; scoring continues independently of HUD visibility.

Loss, bank and SHIFT feedback must preserve the view and leave priority to immediate control and Drive By Ear guidance.

Reflow around the player.

07 / Progression

Earned should look earned.

Shipped state language

Colour reinforces progression but never replaces the label. Trophy Road connects rewards from START to FINISH. Distinguish earned, next, locked and selected states through shape and text.

Vehicleblue-100 → blue-500
Trackgreen-200 → green-500
Feature / perkyellow-100 → yellow-400
Scoringpink-100 → pink-200

Details stay close to the reward

Reward details use a contextual modal layer near the selected road row; clicking outside also closes it. The detail close button uses --turn-orange-500. Preserve the return point inside Achievements.

Motion means availability

Locked perks stay still. Unlocked perks may get the attention wiggle, and selecting a car with an active perk gives the short confirmation wiggle. Reduced motion keeps the state readable without the wiggle.

08 / Accessibility

Part of the control system.

Screen reader, Drive By Ear, keyboard and touch routes share the same product. A visual improvement must preserve the state and timing those routes depend on.

Focus

See where you are

This reference uses Ink focus rings with Paper separation. The cyan focus-role token’s sRGB fallback has only 1.58:1 contrast against Paper; check the actual component before reusing it. The shared production token is unchanged by this reference.

Names & states

Say what changes

Use native buttons, fieldsets, radios, checkboxes and disclosures. Keep accessible names, selection, expanded state and visible symbols aligned. Icon-only close actions need a useful name.

Input ownership

One active context

Driving keys belong to the race surface. Forms and open dialogs own their input. Release held actions on blur or when a modal opens; hidden overlays must not block driving.

Non-visual play

Protect the audio channel

Drive By Ear and Blank screen are supported ways to play. Keep announcements short and event-driven. Decorative score updates must not flood speech or interrupt pace notes.

Motion

Keep meaning when motion stops

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Simplify wiggles, pulses and transitions; retain text, state and position. Avoid adding perpetual motion to documentation examples.

Reflow

Keep the whole task reachable

Check 200% text, narrow and short viewports, both orientations and both handedness settings. Verify the actual rendered touch targets and focus visibility near sticky controls.

The reference’s focus and form examples are corrected locally. Production accessibility changes require their own component review and validation. Physical device and assistive-technology checks remain part of acceptance.

09 / Handoff & sources

Start from what ships.

Read current main and turn/AGENTS.md before changing a component. The source links below point to main; the review date records the baseline checked for this edition.

A component handoff needs

  1. Purpose and source
  2. Anatomy and semantic roles
  3. Default, active, unavailable and error states
  4. Responsive and handedness behaviour
  5. Keyboard, focus and announcement rules
  6. Reviewed release and known limitations

Working in parallel

Use a branch and PR per task, with clear ownership of surfaces. Recheck main before review and merge. Coordinate changes to shared tokens, release metadata and the same component files.

TURN LAB experiments stay separate from production TURN. Update the production build and generated release identities together when delivering a game change.

Known limitations of this edition

Portrait ergonomics are being refined. Some gameplay state colours remain local values. The race orientation hint and temporary menu notice use different wording. Simplified diagrams do not certify game layout or touch-target sizes.

Legacy & migration

Use canonical --turn-* roles for new work. Existing --m8-* and short aliases support shipped components. Keep Ink, Paper and Yellow owned by design-tokens.css; styles.css now takes these values from the canonical aliases. Refactor when work reaches the component; do not churn working gameplay or accessibility behaviour to remove an old name.

Earlier achievement proposal is historical exploration, not the production specification.
