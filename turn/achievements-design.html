04 · Product rules

A small system with room to grow.

TURN owns the definitions and local progress first. Native platform achievements can later mirror the same stable identifiers.

Recognition

Use the familiar grammar

Icon, memorable name, plain requirement, point value, progress and a persistent unlocked state.

Fair criteria

Whole-lap requirement

TRUST YOUR EARS starts only when Blank screen mode is active before the start line and remains active until a valid lap completes.

No power advantage

Status, goals and cosmetics

Achievement points show completion. Future rewards should be cosmetic or choice-expanding, not stronger car performance.

Accessibility

Equivalent feedback

Text, icon and spoken status convey the same result. Motion and sound are supplementary and respect existing preferences.

Rarity

Do not invent percentages

Show designed point value now. Add real completion rarity only when TURN has trustworthy aggregate player data.

Scope

Base collection first

Start with roughly 10–15 durable achievements across racing, technique, exploration, collection and ways to play.

05 · Announcement order

Protect the race audio hierarchy.

The order is deterministic so an achievement never masks information needed to drive or understand the lap result.

  1. Finish and validate the lap.
  2. Present and speak the existing lap result.
  3. Unlock the achievement in persistent storage.
  4. After the lap result completes, show the toast and announce: “Achievement unlocked. Trust Your Ears.”
  5. Mark the Achievements menu button with NEW 1 until the collection is opened.

Recommended first release

Add the green ACHIEVEMENTS entry on Home and in the staged pre-race menu, ship a 10–15 item base collection, and use TRUST YOUR EARS as the first implemented achievement. Keep the modal unavailable during an active timed lap; surface new unlocks through the post-lap toast and the menu badge.
