ROLLER
COASTER

A dense wooden racing track inspired by classic Scandinavian wooden coasters: a long lift, one big drop, airtime hills and a forest of timber supports. No loops. No inversion.

  • Touch: LEFT / RIGHT / GAS / BRAKE.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD; Space brakes.
  • Gravity matters: climbs cost speed and drops give it back.
  • This lab can use over/under crossings; production TURN would need 3D-aware nearest-track logic before this exact layout graduates.
